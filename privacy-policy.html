Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

Ascent is an on-device expedition desk for one hike at a time. There is no Ascent account server and no Ascent cloud backend. Trip plans, field clips, films, and Trail Credits stay in the app sandbox on this iPhone or iPad unless you choose Save to Photos or the iOS share sheet.

Who we are

Ascent is published as a lifestyle field app. Questions go through in-app Support.

Data stored on this device

  • Trip title, date, meeting point, gear checks, checkpoints, and party names you type.
  • Camera clips, imported photo and video copies, microphone voiceovers, and films you export.
  • A local one-tap session name and identifier so the app can return you to the desk.
  • Trail Credit balance, spend records, and Apple StoreKit transaction identifiers after a purchase is verified.

Login

Sign-in is one tap on this device. Ascent does not offer Sign in with Apple and does not collect a password. Log Out ends the local session and leaves trips, clips, and credits on the device. Delete Account removes the local session and the user data written by that session on this installation.

Permissions

  • Camera is requested only after you tap Record Clip.
  • Import Photos opens the system photo picker and does not request full library access. Photo Library add is requested only after you tap Save Film to Photos or Save Summary Card to Photos.
  • Microphone is requested only after you tap Record Clip, Record Mic, or a field microphone. Speech recognition is requested only when you use that microphone for dictation. You can always type instead.
  • Notifications use the system dialog after you reach the main desk. Ascent does not request tracking and does not read the advertising identifier.

Purchases

Trail Credits are Apple consumable in-app purchases. Apple processes the payment. A new local session may receive a starter balance on this device; that gift is not an App Store product. Credits live on this device and are not restored after you delete the app, use Clear All Data, or Delete Account. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Sample content

Sample expeditions, portraits, and preset party names are local demonstration content. They are not a live networked social service and are not a public roster.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a trip, delete a clip, Clear All Data, or Delete Account in Settings. Those actions remove the matching local records and media files on this installation. Spent or unused credits cannot be recovered after a clear.

Contact

Use in-app Support.
